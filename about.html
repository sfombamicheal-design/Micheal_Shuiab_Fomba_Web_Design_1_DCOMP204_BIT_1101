ABOUT STUDENTSTAY

StudentStay helps students find affordable, safe, and comfortable accommodation near their schools and universities.

WHO WE ARE

StudentStay is a student accommodation platform designed to help students find suitable places to live while pursuing their education.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make finding student accommodation easier, safer, and more affordable.

Our Goal

Our goal is to connect students with suitable accommodation and help them make informed decisions about where to live.
